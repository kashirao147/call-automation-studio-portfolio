# Call Automation Studio: Entity and Conversion Audit

Updated: 29 October 2026

## Canonical business identity

**Call Automation Studio** is a custom AI voice-agent service provider. We design, build, integrate and optimise AI phone agents for business call answering, lead capture, appointment booking, missed-call follow-up and CRM workflow automation.

The business is not a generic self-service software product, marketplace, or directory. Each page should lead a prospective client to discuss a custom build through [the contact section](https://callautomationstudio.com/#contact).

## Site-wide corrections completed

- Replaced the legacy **AI Automation Studio** entity label across all public HTML and machine-readable content with **Call Automation Studio**.
- Rewrote homepage title, description, social metadata and structured data around custom AI voice-agent services.
- Added explicit `ProfessionalService`, service-type and offer-catalog context for custom agent builds and call-workflow automation.
- Updated `llms.txt` and the HTML sitemap so AI systems receive the same organisation name and service model.
- Kept the primary domain as `https://callautomationstudio.com/`; legacy hosts redirect to it.

## Content rules for future pages

1. Use **Call Automation Studio** exactly; do not introduce “AI Call Studio” or the old brand name.
2. Describe capabilities as services we design or build for a client, rather than features of a self-serve platform.
3. Do not claim a feature is included unless it is available in the proposed custom build and agreed scope.
4. Keep the canonical URL, clear page title, unique meta description, `Article`/`FAQPage` schema where appropriate, and a visible `/#contact` CTA.
5. For Australia-targeted pages, use Australian spelling and clearly describe the local workflow or industry served.

## Google result follow-up

After the production deployment, request indexing for the homepage and priority service pages in Google Search Console. Search snippets and AI Overviews are generated by Google and can take time to refresh; the goal of these changes is to give Google one consistent, accurate source of truth.
