How do Retell AI, Vapi, and OpenAI fit into a voice agent stack?

Retell AI and Vapi are voice-agent orchestration platforms, while OpenAI models can provide language reasoning or realtime speech capabilities inside a broader phone system. A production integration connects the selected platform to telephony, business tools, knowledge, analytics, and human handoff. The best architecture depends on control, latency, provider flexibility, compliance needs, and operating cost.

Platform roles and architecture decisions

Retell offers a managed voice-agent layer with model, voice, telephony, knowledge, and quality options. Vapi is attractive when a team wants to compose speech recognition, language models, voices, and carriers across providers. OpenAI can power reasoning within either style of stack, or support a custom realtime implementation when the engineering team wants direct control.

The architecture should be chosen after mapping the call, not before it. A simple FAQ and transfer line needs different tools from an agent that checks live availability, writes to a CRM, handles payment-adjacent data, or runs thousands of outbound calls. Provider choice does not replace conversation design, secure tool permissions, fallback logic, and monitoring.

Integration layers we test

  • Webhooks and tools for CRM, calendars, messaging, and custom APIs.

From prototype to production

A narrow prototype proves the conversation and integration path. Test cases then cover expected calls, incomplete answers, silence, background noise, repeated questions, unavailable tools, and requests outside scope. Limited production traffic provides real latency and outcome data before wider rollout.
