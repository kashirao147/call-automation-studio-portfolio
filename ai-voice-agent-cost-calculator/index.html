Short answer: cost is normally calculated as monthly call minutes × the platform’s per-minute stack, plus telephony, phone numbers, subscriptions and optional add-ons. For 1,000 calls lasting three minutes, that means 3,000 billable voice minutes. A lower-cost configuration may be around a few hundred dollars per month, while premium models, voices, international calling and higher concurrency can increase the estimate substantially.

How does an AI voice agent work?

An AI voice agent connects a phone call to speech recognition, a language model, a synthetic voice and business tools. It listens to the caller, converts speech to text, decides what to say or do, speaks the response, and can update a CRM, book an appointment, send an SMS or transfer the call.

  1. Caller speaks
    Telephony connects the live call.
  2. Speech becomes text
    STT transcribes the caller in real time.
  3. AI reasons and acts
    The LLM uses prompts, knowledge and tools.
  4. Voice responds
    TTS speaks naturally and the loop repeats.

Five AI voice agent platforms explained

Retell AI

Retell combines voice infrastructure with selectable voices, language models, telephony and add-ons. It suits teams that want managed orchestration with flexible components and transparent per-minute line items.

Vapi

Vapi provides the orchestration and hosting layer while letting developers combine separate STT, LLM, TTS and telephony providers. Its advertised platform fee does not include most model-provider costs.

Bland AI

Bland uses bundled per-minute plans that include its core LLM, STT and TTS. Start has no platform fee; Build and Scale add monthly fees in exchange for lower usage rates and higher limits.

ElevenLabs Agents

ElevenLabs uses subscription tiers with included call minutes and concurrency. It is attractive where natural voice quality and the ElevenLabs voice ecosystem matter; LLM and telephony costs remain separate.

Telnyx Voice AI

Telnyx combines orchestration, hosted STT and hosted TTS at one voice-engine rate, then adds LLM tokens and carrier telephony. Owning the telecom layer can make its US planning estimate especially competitive.

How this calculator estimates AI voice agent cost

Monthly minutes = calls per month × average call duration. The calculator then applies each platform’s public pricing model. Usage charges, telephony, subscriptions, phone numbers and concurrency are itemized where applicable. Rates live in a separate editable database so pricing can be updated without rewriting the article.

The result is a planning estimate, not a provider quote. Actual invoices can change with token usage, silence, call rounding, destination, carrier, taxes, volume discounts, premium voices, transfers and custom enterprise contracts.

Which AI voice agent platform should you choose?

Choose based on the full operating model, not only the lowest card. Retell is a balanced managed stack; Vapi offers developer-level composability; Bland simplifies core usage into a bundled rate; ElevenLabs emphasizes voice quality and its voice ecosystem; Telnyx integrates AI with carrier infrastructure. Reliability, latency, analytics, compliance, workflow integration and support can matter more than a small per-minute difference.

AI voice agent pricing FAQ

How much does an AI voice agent cost per minute?

There is no single price. A complete stack may combine platform, STT, LLM, TTS and telephony charges. Use the calculator with your own call volume and configuration to estimate the effective per-minute cost.

Which platform is cheapest for 3,000 minutes?

It depends on voice, model, country and subscription. With economy settings and US calling, an integrated Telnyx configuration can estimate lower than many stitched-together stacks, but your operational requirements may favor another provider.

Does the estimate include phone calls and phone numbers?

Yes. The calculator includes a telephony planning rate and monthly phone-number cost. US defaults use current public benchmarks; other destinations should be replaced with your carrier quote for procurement decisions.

Why can Vapi cost more than its advertised $0.05 per minute?

Vapi’s public hosting rate excludes model-provider costs. Speech recognition, the language model, synthetic voice and telephony are added separately unless you bring your own provider keys.

Are these prices guaranteed?

No. They are educational estimates based on public rates checked on August 21, 2026. Provider pricing and usage patterns can change, so verify the linked official pages before purchasing.
