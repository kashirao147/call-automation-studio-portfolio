Is an AI receptionist cheaper than a human receptionist?

Usually for phone coverage, yes—but it is not a like-for-like replacement. A human receptionist can handle visitors, emotional judgment and physical office tasks. AI is designed for repeatable calls such as answering FAQs, booking appointments, taking messages, qualifying leads and routing callers. AI receptionist vs human receptionist: quick comparison

Key takeaway: AI usually wins on repeatable phone coverage, concurrency, consistency and availability. Humans win on empathy, physical presence, novel situations and nuanced judgment. For many service businesses, the most practical design is hybrid rather than complete replacement.

How the AI receptionist cost calculator works

Monthly human cost = salary × (1 + payroll and benefits overhead).

Monthly AI minutes = calls per day × working days per month × average call duration.

Monthly AI cost = monthly AI minutes × AI cost per minute + fixed monthly AI fee.

Potential saving = monthly human cost − monthly AI cost. The savings percentage divides that difference by the loaded human cost. Missed-call opportunity is shown separately because it is uncertain revenue, not a guaranteed saving.

What affects the true cost of AI and human receptionists?

Human cost may extend beyond salary to payroll taxes, benefits, recruitment, training, equipment, leave cover and after-hours answering. AI cost may include call minutes, a subscription, telephony, phone numbers, premium voices, CRM or scheduling integrations, setup, maintenance and human transfer time. Use your actual provider quote and payroll data for a purchasing decision.

Should you choose AI, a human receptionist or a hybrid?

AI-first

Best when calls are repetitive and action-oriented.

  • Appointment booking
  • FAQs and hours
  • Lead qualification
  • Messages and routing

Human-first

Best when the role depends on presence or judgment.

  • Walk-in visitors
  • Sensitive conversations
  • Unusual requests
  • Broad office administration

Hybrid

Best when you want efficiency without removing human escalation.

  • AI handles first response
  • Human takes complex calls
  • AI covers overflow
  • AI answers after hours

AI receptionist cost FAQ

How much does an AI receptionist cost per month?

Pricing varies by call volume, provider and features. Use your per-minute or subscription quote in the calculator. Public market guides commonly show a wide range, so your own call minutes provide a more useful estimate than a single headline price.

Can an AI receptionist completely replace a human?

It can replace or automate routine phone tasks for some businesses, but it cannot greet visitors or perform physical work and should escalate sensitive, risky or unusual calls. A hybrid model is often safer.

What should be included in human receptionist cost?

Start with salary and add employer payroll costs, benefits, leave coverage, recruitment, training and equipment where applicable. The calculator provides an overhead percentage field for these costs.

How accurate is this savings calculator?

The formulas are deterministic, but accuracy depends on your inputs. Use actual payroll figures, connected call minutes and a current AI provider quote. Missed-call revenue is a potential opportunity, not guaranteed income.

Is an AI receptionist suitable for healthcare or legal businesses?

Only with appropriate privacy, security, consent, data-retention and escalation controls. Confirm the selected vendor’s contractual and regulatory suitability before handling sensitive information.
